# IDR-2026-AA-001 — MBB-Style Redesign Blueprint

## Important note on source quality
The uploaded slide images appear fully white/blank in this environment, so source text and existing charts are not recoverable from the provided files. This blueprint therefore provides a **slide-by-slide, data-dense replacement structure** you can paste into PowerPoint/Google Slides and populate with your source numbers.

## Design system (use globally)
- Palette: Deep navy `#0B1F3A`, slate `#4A5568`, accent teal `#00A3A3`, warning amber `#F59E0B`, risk red `#D64545`, background `#F7FAFC`.
- Typeface: Inter/Calibri; title 30–34 pt, section 22–24 pt, body 14–16 pt, chart labels 10–12 pt.
- Layout discipline: 12-column grid, max 40 words per slide, “so-what” subtitle under every headline.
- Chart standards: Show period, source, unit, sample size, and confidence interval where relevant.

## Slide-by-slide rebuild (30-slide executive narrative)

### 1) Cover / Decision Ask
- **Headline:** “Approve FY26 transformation plan to deliver +$XM EBITDA and -Y% risk exposure in 12 months.”
- **Visual:** One hero KPI ribbon (3–5 metrics) + executive decision box.
- **Data blocks:** Baseline vs target metrics; owner; date; required approvals.

### 2) Executive Summary (One-slide synthesis)
- **Headline:** “Three levers drive >80% of value; sequencing is the critical success factor.”
- **Visual:** 3-column “situation–insight–action–impact” matrix.
- **Data blocks:** Value at stake by lever, probability-adjusted outcomes, investment required.

### 3) Context and Mandate
- **Headline:** “External pressure and internal complexity require a step-change, not incremental fixes.”
- **Visual:** Timeline of market/regulatory/internal events.
- **Data blocks:** Demand growth, margin compression, service-level trend.

### 4) Problem Statement / Current State
- **Headline:** “Performance gap is structural: cost-to-serve +XX% vs peers and conversion -YY pts.”
- **Visual:** Waterfall from peer median to current performance gap.
- **Data blocks:** Peer benchmark set, quartile ranges, internal segment splits.

### 5) Methodology and Evidence Base
- **Headline:** “Findings are triangulated across financials, operations, and customer voice.”
- **Visual:** Evidence triangle (quant, qual, external).
- **Data blocks:** Sample sizes, date ranges, data reliability score.

### 6) Market Size & Growth
- **Headline:** “Core market grows +A% CAGR, but growth pools are concentrated in two subsegments.”
- **Visual:** Stacked area TAM/SAM/SOM over 5 years.
- **Data blocks:** CAGR by segment, share shifts, volatility bands.

### 7) Competitive Landscape
- **Headline:** “Top 3 competitors are winning through speed, not price.”
- **Visual:** Bubble chart (share vs margin vs growth).
- **Data blocks:** Competitor KPIs, capabilities heatmap.

### 8) Customer Segmentation
- **Headline:** “Two high-value segments account for 68% of future profit pool.”
- **Visual:** Segment profitability bars + needs-based archetypes.
- **Data blocks:** LTV/CAC, retention, NPS by segment.

### 9) Voice of Customer Insights
- **Headline:** “Primary churn driver is inconsistent delivery at key journey moments.”
- **Visual:** Journey map with pain-point intensity.
- **Data blocks:** Complaint taxonomy, CSAT/NPS by touchpoint.

### 10) Revenue Bridge (Past 12–24 months)
- **Headline:** “Volume mix and discounting explain 90% of topline underperformance.”
- **Visual:** Revenue variance bridge vs plan.
- **Data blocks:** Price, mix, volume, leakage components.

### 11) Cost Structure Diagnostic
- **Headline:** “Overhead inflation and process fragmentation create avoidable $X cost burden.”
- **Visual:** Cost tree + Pareto of top cost drivers.
- **Data blocks:** Fixed vs variable split, unit cost variance.

### 12) Productivity Benchmark
- **Headline:** “Frontline productivity trails top quartile by 18–25%.”
- **Visual:** Boxplot by team/site/function.
- **Data blocks:** Throughput, utilization, rework rates.

### 13) Process Bottleneck Analysis
- **Headline:** “Two bottlenecks create 60% of cycle-time delay.”
- **Visual:** Sankey/process flow with queue times.
- **Data blocks:** Cycle-time decomposition, wait-time heatmap.

### 14) Technology and Data Maturity
- **Headline:** “Capability gap is not tooling count; it is integration and governance.”
- **Visual:** Capability maturity radar (current vs target).
- **Data blocks:** System fragmentation index, data quality KPIs.

### 15) Risk and Controls
- **Headline:** “Control failures are concentrated in 3 high-severity failure modes.”
- **Visual:** Risk matrix (impact × likelihood) + control coverage.
- **Data blocks:** Incident frequency, loss history, control effectiveness.

### 16) Root Cause Synthesis
- **Headline:** “Five root causes explain recurring underperformance across functions.”
- **Visual:** Fishbone with quantified contribution.
- **Data blocks:** Root-cause contribution percentages.

### 17) Strategic Options
- **Headline:** “Option B maximizes NPV with manageable execution risk.”
- **Visual:** 2×2 (value vs feasibility) with option cards.
- **Data blocks:** Capex/opex, payback, risk score.

### 18) Recommended Strategy
- **Headline:** “Adopt phased transformation focused on commercial excellence + operating simplification.”
- **Visual:** Strategy house (north star, pillars, enablers).
- **Data blocks:** KPI targets by pillar.

### 19) Value Creation Plan
- **Headline:** “Total value at stake: $XM gross, $YM net after implementation cost.”
- **Visual:** Lever waterfall and cumulative benefit curve.
- **Data blocks:** Confidence-weighted benefits, owner by lever.

### 20) Initiative Deep Dive 1 (Commercial)
- **Headline:** “Pricing and mix program yields fastest payback (≤2 quarters).”
- **Visual:** Price corridor and elasticity scatter.
- **Data blocks:** SKU/customer-level uplift assumptions.

### 21) Initiative Deep Dive 2 (Operations)
- **Headline:** “Lean redesign reduces cycle time by 30–40% and defects by 20%.”
- **Visual:** Before/after process metrics dashboard.
- **Data blocks:** Pilot results, control groups.

### 22) Initiative Deep Dive 3 (Digital/Data)
- **Headline:** “Automation and decision intelligence unlock scalable productivity.”
- **Visual:** Automation pipeline funnel.
- **Data blocks:** Use-case ROI, adoption ramp.

### 23) Organization & Operating Model
- **Headline:** “Accountability reset is required to sustain gains.”
- **Visual:** RACI heatmap + decision rights chart.
- **Data blocks:** Span/layer analysis, role redesign impacts.

### 24) Change Management & Capability Build
- **Headline:** “Transformation risk is mainly behavioral; build capabilities in wave-based sprints.”
- **Visual:** Change curve + training roadmap.
- **Data blocks:** Adoption KPI targets, completion thresholds.

### 25) Implementation Roadmap (0–30–90–180–365)
- **Headline:** “Front-load no-regret moves while de-risking structural bets.”
- **Visual:** Gantt with milestones and critical path.
- **Data blocks:** Milestone gates, dependency map.

### 26) Financial Plan and Funding
- **Headline:** “Program is self-funding by Q3 under base case.”
- **Visual:** Cash flow profile and payback chart.
- **Data blocks:** Investment phasing, sensitivity bands.

### 27) Scenario and Sensitivity Analysis
- **Headline:** “Downside case preserves >60% of base value if trigger actions execute on time.”
- **Visual:** Tornado chart + scenario table.
- **Data blocks:** Key assumptions and thresholds.

### 28) Governance and PMO Cadence
- **Headline:** “Weekly performance cockpit and monthly steering unlock execution speed.”
- **Visual:** Governance pyramid + KPI cockpit mock.
- **Data blocks:** Meeting cadence, escalation protocol.

### 29) Decisions Required Today
- **Headline:** “Five decisions unlock immediate mobilization.”
- **Visual:** Decision log with owners and deadlines.
- **Data blocks:** Decision impact, risks of delay.

### 30) Appendix Index / Backup Navigation
- **Headline:** “Backup analyses available by workstream, metric, and data source.”
- **Visual:** Hyperlinked appendix map.
- **Data blocks:** Source library, assumptions register.

- 1 executive waterfall (value bridge)
- 2 benchmark visuals (peer vs internal)
- 2 distribution visuals (boxplots/histograms)
- 1 scenario tornado
- 1 initiative portfolio bubble chart
- 1 implementation Gantt with stage gates
- 1 PMO KPI cockpit

## Commentary style guide
- Start each page with one sentence answer (“therefore…”).
- Separate fact, inference, and recommendation visually.
- Use action verbs + quantified outcomes (“reduce,” “unlock,” “de-risk”).
